## The build

- **What I built:** A dashboard that shows why B2B SaaS customers churn in their first 90 days
- **Tool used:** Replit
- **Shareable link:** https://90-day-saa-s-churn-dashboard.replit.app/

## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** What is the main reason for customer to churning and the customers who are at the risk of Churn, the trend is also helping to understand it better
- **What confused them:** Not confusing , lot of information. Mix feeling where to act first in the dashboard.
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** The reason of the churn is crucial to act
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** The partner understood the purpose but she didn't get users are churning for the first 90 day, Also first action point where to act was not clear to her.The prototype is testing whether you can move the retention curve which is not explicitly understood by the partner.
